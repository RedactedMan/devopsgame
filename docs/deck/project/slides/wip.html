This is the First Way, measured. Same nine people, same work arriving, only the WIP sliders move. Tightening to 0.4 times the starting limits ships 39% more at 39% lower lead time: 168 items at 38 hours against 121 at 62. Loosening never helps. (In free play, where work arrives faster, it is 43% more at 26% lower.) Why: every deploy moves trunk, and the more that's in flight, the more of it drifts, collides with other work in the same areas of the code, and needs rework or a rebase. Doubling WIP more than doubles rework. Ask the room: who felt the urge to open the limits up when the backlog grew? That urge is the intuitive wrong move, and it's the same urge that says "spin up ten agents".
