ONE RUN OF THE GAME

50 sim-days

About 13 minutes at 1× speed. About 8 minutes at the recommended pace, plus however long you pause.

Here's the shape of the next 45 minutes. I talk for about ten, then you play for fifteen, and the last fifteen are about what you just did. One run of the game is 50 simulated days: 4,000 ticks at 5 ticks per second, so 13 minutes 20 seconds at 1×, 6:40 at 2×, 3:20 at 4×. Pausing costs nothing. The 15-minute play block leaves room for one careful run, or a quick second run for anyone who finishes early. The leaderboard keeps each person's best. If the room is slow to join, borrow from the principles block, not the play block. The play time is the part that teaches.
