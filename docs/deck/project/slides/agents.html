WHAT THE SIM MEASURED · 2

Agents only pay once review can keep up

Add 8 agents, nobody moved to Review

−14%

Worse than doing nothing. Their output drains the attention review runs on.

Move 1 person to Review, then add 4 agents

38h→13h

Lead time cut by two thirds, and it keeps up on every seed measured. Past about four agents, it turns down again.

And you can over-correct: move two implementers to Review with no backfill and you ship 28% less.

Flow State headless sweep: 12 seeds × 50 sim-days at the session's 4 items a day · docs/HIRING_AND_ATTENTION.md §10

This is the slide the game was built for. In the game, agents can't staff Review. Hover over Review's staffing row and it says: "Agents can produce the work. They cannot be the judgment about it." Every agent also draws on the per-shift attention budget, because a person has to read what it wrote. So eight agents added with nobody moved to Review ship 14% less than the untouched line. Move one person to Review first and four agents take lead time from 38 hours to 13, and that line clears the work: it is the play that keeps up on every seed we measured. It ships only 13% more, because in the session there is only so much work to ship; the win shows up as speed and as keeping up. (In free play, where the work never runs out, the same move is worth +35% shipped.) The agents weren't weak; they were blocked, and what blocked them was the one station they can't be pointed at. The order you do things in is the whole decision. Ask: who saw "my agents aren't doing anything"? What did you do about it?
