WHERE THE ATTENTION GOES

Humans at both ends, agents in the middle

HUMAN

Plan

Intent, acceptance criteria, and what not to touch, written down

AGENTS

Build

Cheap, fast, parallel, and held to a WIP limit

HUMAN

Review

Read it, understand it, own the merge

Every case we just looked at ended by adding attention at one of these two ends.

This is the model I've landed on. Human attention is the scarce resource, so spend it where it has the most leverage: at the start, turning a vague want into an intent an agent can't misread, and at the end, judging whether what came back is right. The middle is where agents earn their keep: lots of parallel doing. It still obeys the First Way, so hold agents to a WIP limit set by your review capacity, not by your budget. Replit added planning mode: the left end. AWS added peer review: the right end. Amazon retail added senior sign-off: the right end again.
