Two minutes, no more. Work arrives in the backlog and flows through five stations. Every deploy advances trunk, and anything still in flight drifts behind it: teal is healthy, amber is drifting, rust is stale. You have four levers. Don't explain what the right settings are. Resist it, even if someone asks. Two results. You keep up if 10 or fewer items are unfinished, waiting or in flight, when the clock stops; the top bar shows Unfinished against that line all game. The leaderboard ranks by score, which is quality-weighted: every shipped item counts for the true quality it shipped at, so shipping stale work anyway pays less than rebasing it. Keeping up is possible, and not by default: leave the line alone and you finish with dozens unfinished. Mention the attention meter in the top bar. It'll matter. Anyone lost on what the colours, the "5u" or the blue bar mean: the ? in the top bar has a two-minute tour and a key, and hovering or tapping an item says what it is. It explains the screen, not the answer, and it pauses their clock while it's open.
