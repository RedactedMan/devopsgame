Start a visible 15-minute timer. Timing math: a run is 4,000 ticks, and the game advances 5 ticks a second at 1×. Days 1–10 at 1× take 2:40; days 11–50 at 2× take 5:20. So about 8 minutes of game time, and most first-time players pause enough to land at 10–13 minutes. Fast players will get a second run in; the leaderboard keeps each person's best. Walk the room. The two things to listen for: "my agents aren't doing anything" and "everything's gone red". Don't explain either one; ask "what do you think is happening?" and write down what people say. You'll use their words in the debrief. Give a 3-minute and a 1-minute warning.
