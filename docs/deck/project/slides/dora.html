Forsgren, Humble, Kim: Accelerate (2018) · dora.dev

How do you know any of this works? DORA's four keys: two measure throughput, two measure stability. Ten years of survey data say high performers are better at all four at once. Going fast and staying safe aren't a trade-off; they come from the same practices. Keep this slide in mind for the debrief. When agents arrived, individual output went up a lot, and these four numbers mostly didn't move. That gap is what the second half of the talk is about.
