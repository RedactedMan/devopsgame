Work in progress sets your lead time

LITTLE'S LAW

lead time =
WIP ÷ throughput

Start more work without finishing faster, and everything takes longer.

  • Limit work in progress
  • Shrink the batch size
  • Make the queues visible
  • Stop starting, start finishing
Little's Law is just arithmetic, and it holds for any stable system: average lead time equals average work in progress divided by average throughput. If throughput is fixed, the only way to cut lead time is to have less in flight. Software adds a twist a factory doesn't have: unfinished work doesn't just wait, it decays. Every deploy moves trunk, and every open branch drifts further behind it. More in flight means more drift and more merge conflicts. That decay is the thing the game makes visible. Don't give away the answer. Leave it at "there's a right amount of WIP", and let the game show them where it is.
