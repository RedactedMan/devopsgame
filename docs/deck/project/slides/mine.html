FROM MY OWN TEAMS

What I've changed this year

PLANNING

Talk with the team and make a decision on what we want to accomplish. Make a plan with the AI on what the goal is, and record the plan in an md file for larger changes.

REVIEW

Get a basic understanding of the change and how risky it is before going in depth with the AI on a code review. AI code reviews are often focused on the trees (mechanical issues) and not the forest (why these changes are being done). Did implementing the plan reveal new information? Should we start over with a new plan, or clean up the existing MR and land on main?

A MISS

Spending significant time, tokens, and discussion on work that is lower priority or not needed at all. The AI often will flag things that are tangentially related to the work we are trying to get done. Don't let it distract you, or let it drive how you are spending your time.
