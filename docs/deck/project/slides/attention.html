THE ANSWER THE GAME GIVES

Human attention is the constraint.

Agents add doing. Only people add judgment. And money can't buy more attention.

Here's the answer to the question from before the game. When writing code gets nearly free, the Three Ways don't go away. The constraint moves. It used to be the typing. Now it's the thinking on either side of it: deciding what to build, and judging whether what got built is right. The game's design rule is "money buys capacity, attention buys judgment, money cannot buy attention". You can buy as many agent seats as you like. You can't buy another hour of a senior engineer's careful reading. Now let's check whether the real world agrees with a game.
